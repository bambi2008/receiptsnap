## Product Hunt Launch

### Tagline
🧾 Stop losing thousands in tax deductions. Snap a receipt. AI does the rest.

### Description
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm a freelancer who's been tracking receipts in a shoebox for years. Every March, I'd spend hours digging through crumpled paper trying to figure out what I could deduct. So I built the tool I wished I had.

**ReceiptSnap is an iOS app that turns any receipt into a tax deduction in 3 seconds:**

📸 **Snap** — Open the app, tap the shutter. Or pick from your photo library. No menus, no friction.

🤖 **AI Reads** — Apple Vision OCR extracts vendor, amount, date from the receipt. On-device. Works offline.

📊 **Auto-Categorize** — Every receipt gets the right IRS Schedule C category (Meals 50%, Home Office, Travel, Software, etc). Your CPA will love you.

📤 **Export** — One tap → categorized PDF or CSV. Ready for tax time.

**Pricing:**
- Free: 50 receipts forever
- Pro: $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr

**Privacy-first:** All data stays on your phone. No server. No account required.

Built for freelancers, by a freelancer. I'd love your feedback! 🧾
