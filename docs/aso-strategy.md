## Ratings & Reviews Strategy

1. **Prompt timing:** After 10th successful scan (likely ~2 weeks of use)
2. **Prompt method:** Native `SKStoreReviewController` (Apple's API)
3. **Pre-prompt condition:** Only show if user has scanned in the last 7 days
4. **Happiness check:** Only show after a confirmation/save action (not after an error)
5. **Never show:** On cold launch, during onboarding, after a crash
