--- Description ---

Organize business receipt records before tax time arrives.

Snap a photo. On-device OCR extracts the vendor, amount, and date, then suggests a common business expense category for you to review. Export records as PDF or CSV for yourself or your tax professional. Try 50 receipts free.

Your Receipts. Your Records. Organized.

ReceiptSnap is an on-device receipt scanner built for freelancers and self-employed professionals. Keep receipt details together throughout the year, review every extracted field, and prepare clean records for tax time.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Snap a photo of any business receipt
2. AI extracts vendor, amount, date — automatically
3. Review the suggested expense category (Advertising, Meals, Travel, Software, and more)
4. Export a clean PDF or CSV for your records or tax professional

BUILT FOR FREELANCERS
ReceiptSnap uses practical expense labels such as Meals, Travel and Software/Tools. Suggestions are for organization only; you stay in control and can change every field before saving.
