SnapDeduct Privacy Policy

Effective: June 25, 2026

Our Privacy Promise

SnapDeduct stores all your data exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any of your receipts, financial information, mileage logs, income data, or tax calculations.

Data We Collect

Nothing. SnapDeduct does not have a server. There is no backend. There is no analytics SDK. There is no advertising SDK. All data lives in a local database (Hive) on your iPhone or Mac.

Data on Your Device

SnapDeduct stores the following locally only:

  • Receipt images (camera or gallery)
  • OCR-extracted vendor names, amounts, dates, and categories
  • Mileage trip logs (date, miles, purpose, destination)
  • Home office square footage
  • Estimated annual income (for quarterly tax estimates)
  • Subscription status (via Apple's StoreKit framework)
  • User preferences (theme, onboarding completion)

Third-Party Access

None. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data processors. The only external communication is:

  • Apple Vision OCR — runs entirely on-device for receipt text recognition. No images leave your device.
  • Apple StoreKit — handles subscriptions and purchase verification through Apple's secure framework.
  • URL opening — if you tap Privacy Policy or Terms of Service links, they open in your browser.

Camera & Photo Library

SnapDeduct requests camera and photo library access solely to capture and store receipt images on your device. Images are never transmitted anywhere.

Children

SnapDeduct is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will notify you within the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
