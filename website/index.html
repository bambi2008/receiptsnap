FAQ

You ask. We answer.

Is ReceiptSnap really free?
Yes. The free tier gives you 50 receipts — enough for most freelancers to try it for a month. Upgrade to Pro ($4.99/mo) for unlimited scans.
Does it work offline?
Yes. All OCR processing runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No internet connection needed to scan receipts.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. ReceiptSnap stores everything on your phone. No server. No cloud. No account. We never see your receipts. Period.
What IRS categories does it support?
We cover all major Schedule C categories: Meals (50% deductible), Travel, Home Office, Supplies, Software & Tools, Phone & Internet, Rent & Workspace, Shipping, Advertising, and Other.
Can I export to my accountant?
Yes. One tap exports all your receipts as a categorized PDF or CSV. Share via email, AirDrop, or save to Files. Your CPA will love you.
