Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ReceiptSnap ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in the ReceiptSnap iOS application (the "App") and on the ReceiptSnap early-access website (the "Website").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Receipt Photos: When you scan a receipt, we process the image using on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract text data (vendor name, amount, date).
  • Receipt Data: Vendor names, amounts, dates, and categories you assign to receipts.
  • Subscription Status: Apple processes purchases. The App reads verified subscription entitlement information on your device; we do not receive or store your payment-card details.
  • Waitlist Email: If you join the Website waitlist, your email address and the source of the signup are submitted to Formspree for delivery to us.

2.2 Information the App Does Not Send to Us

The current App does not include our own analytics, advertising, account, or crash-reporting service. Receipt images, extracted text, saved receipt fields, and the local free-tier count are not transmitted to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core service: scan, categorize, and store your receipts
  • To generate export files (PDF, CSV) at your request
  • To manage your free tier usage (receipt count)
  • To verify subscription access through Apple
  • To send launch updates if you voluntarily join the Website waitlist
  • To respond if you contact support

4. Data Storage and Processing

4.1 On-Device Storage

Receipt images, extracted text, categories, and notes are stored locally in the App's sandbox on your device. Receipt records use Hive local storage. We do not upload this receipt data to our servers. Device passcode, operating-system data protection, backups, and anyone with access to your device may affect its confidentiality.

4.2 OCR Processing

Receipt text extraction is performed on the device using Apple's Vision framework. The App does not send receipt images to us for OCR.

4.3 Website Waitlist

Waitlist submissions are processed by Formspree, an external service provider. Formspree may process request metadata such as IP address and browser information under its own terms and privacy practices. We retain waitlist emails until launch communications are complete or you ask us to delete yours.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Your CPA/Accountant: You may export and share your receipt data as a PDF or CSV file. This is entirely under your control.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or in response to valid legal process.
  • Service Providers: Apple provides payment and subscription services. Formspree processes Website waitlist submissions. Their privacy terms apply to their processing.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • View: All your receipts are visible in the App
  • Edit: You can edit any receipt's details at any time
  • Delete: You can delete individual receipts or clear all data from within the App
  • Export: You can export your data as PDF or CSV at any time
  • Waitlist: You can ask us to remove your waitlist email by contacting support

7. Data Retention

Receipt data remains in local App storage until you delete it or clear all receipt data. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings. We retain Website waitlist emails as described in Section 4.3.

8. Children's Privacy

ReceiptSnap is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
